# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
- **Professional & Academic**: The application uses a precise, formal, and authoritative tone, reflecting its role as a serious tool for academic and interdisciplinary analysis.

## Design Principles
- **Consistency**: We maintain a unified design system, leveraging components from `shadcn/ui`. This ensures that students and instructors have a predictable and reliable experience throughout the application.

## AI Feedback Presentation
- **Action-Oriented**: AI-generated feedback is presented with a focus on specific, actionable steps. The goal is to provide students with clear guidance on how to improve their work in subsequent iterations.

## Data Visualization
- **Simplified & Comparative**: Dashboards prioritize comparing current performance against established benchmarks or previous work. This helps users quickly identify growth trends and areas needing attention.

## Error Handling
- **Informative & Proactive**: When errors occur, the system provides clear, understandable messages. We don't just state that something went wrong; we proactively suggest potential solutions or next steps to the user.
